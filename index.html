Why BabySafe+

"Hey, mom. If you're anything like me, you read every single ingredient before using anything on your baby."

When I was pregnant, I struggled to find baby products in East Africa that I truly trusted. And I kept thinking, why is this so hard?

As a chemical engineer, I understand what goes into these products, and what should be left out. I've spent years working with non-toxic formulas, from skincare to household products. But when it came to my own baby, nothing felt good enough.

So I decided to create my own. That's how BabySafe+ was born, right here in East Africa. Non-toxic, fragrance-free, eco-friendly, made for the most delicate skin.

Created, loved, and trusted by a mom just like you. Welcome to BabySafe+.
